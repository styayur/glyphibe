# Community conduct

Treat participants with respect. Critique ideas and evidence; do not harass,
discriminate, threaten or publish others' private information. Keep discussions
relevant and make room for beginners asking code-comprehension questions.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content and restrict participation for
repeated or serious violations. Reports may be made privately through the
repository's private reporting channel when available, or by asking an owner
for a private contact in a discussion without naming affected participants.
Maintainers should protect reporters' privacy and disclose conflicts of interest.
